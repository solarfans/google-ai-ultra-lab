Google turned Gemini Spark into Gemini Tasks, a cloud agent that keeps working after you lock your phone.
Flip Chat to Task, and Google runs an isolated cloud computer and remote browser, or controls Chrome on your Mac.
It's free in six countries with ten daily schedules. Pro and Ultra unlock fifty schedules, fifteen parallel tasks, plus Gmail and topic monitors.
ChatGPT Dots needs a hundred-dollar Pro plan for team workflows, while Gemini Tasks lives right inside Gmail, Drive, and Chrome.
Here is the big catch: Tasks share a rolling five-hour compute pool. One Ultra user hit the wall in ninety minutes on two heavy jobs.
Plus business calls and Google Pay checkout remain U.S. only, and topic monitors aren't fast enough for instant ticket drops.
The playbook: run Gmail monitors, Workspace reports, and web sweeps in Gemini Tasks, and keep heavy code on your local stack.
